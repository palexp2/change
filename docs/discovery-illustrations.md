# Illustrations du formulaire de découverte

Ressources : `client/public/images/discovery/`.
Les PNG reprennent les références fournies par le client (profils C/O, dimensions,
réseau, côté ouvrant, diamètre). Les huit WebP ont été générés avec l’outil intégré
`imagegen`, puis réduits à 640 px pour le formulaire.

## Prompts

Préfixe commun : Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: small static illustration beside a French greenhouse equipment questionnaire. Create one image of:

site.webp: A farm building and two separate hoop greenhouses, green seedlings inside.

shipping.webp: A small delivery truck carrying plain equipment boxes beside a greenhouse.

network_mobile.webp: A mobile internet router with two antennas and a small cellular signal symbol beside a greenhouse.

guide_pipes.webp: Side elevation of a greenhouse with rolled up side curtain, emphasize three vertical metal guide pipes outside the curtain in dark teal.

furnaces.webp: A suspended rectangular greenhouse unit heater with a circular fan grille and short flue, inside a pale blue greenhouse outline above seedlings.

furnace_wire.webp: A greenhouse heater and small wall thermostat joined by a clearly visible thin control cable. Simplified educational illustration, not a wiring installation diagram.

thermostat.webp: A simple wall mounted backup thermostat with temperature dial, inside a greenhouse outline with seedlings.

irrigation.webp: Two rows of seedlings irrigated by horizontal drip pipes, a small irrigation manifold with two distinct valves feeding the rows.

Suffixe commun : Style references are the attached images, use their flat 2D technical illustration style, white background, very pale cyan greenhouse outlines, teal accents, green seedlings, neutral gray hardware. Clean thick simple strokes, front or side elevation, generous white margins, landscape 3:2 composition. No perspective 3D render, no shadows, no text, no lettering, no logos, no measurements. One cohesive illustration, legible at 220px wide.
